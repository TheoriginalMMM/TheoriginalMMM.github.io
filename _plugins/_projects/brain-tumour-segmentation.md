## Objective

Identify a practical framework for automatic brain-tumour segmentation that met the scientific and technical constraints of the Marc Jeannerod Institute of Cognitive Science.

## Contribution

- Reviewed segmentation approaches for three-dimensional medical images.
- Compared available frameworks and their operational requirements.
- Selected and documented nnU-Net and DeepMedic workflows.
- Produced a reusable tutorial for training models or applying pretrained models.
