## Implemented systems

- Autonomous Pac-Man agents using Markov decision processes, Q-learning and approximate Q-learning.
- Deep reinforcement-learning agents for several Gym environments.
- Comparative experiments to understand exploration, convergence and generalisation across environments.

These projects combined classical decision processes with function approximation and deep learning, providing practical experience in reward design, exploration strategies and evaluation of autonomous agents.
