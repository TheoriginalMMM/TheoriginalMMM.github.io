## Research question

Can a model infer a useful low-dimensional spatial representation directly from high-dimensional wireless channel measurements, while remaining stable across different reconfigurable intelligent surface configurations?

## Approach

- Extract structured features from multi-antenna, multi-subcarrier channel state information.
- Train forward channel-charting functions with temporal triplet constraints.
- Compare RIS-aware sampling, cross-mode regularisation and adversarial domain-invariance strategies.
- Evaluate geometry with trustworthiness, continuity and Kruskal stress.
- Measure RIS-induced cross-mode variability and downstream performance for radio maps and RIS control.

## Research outputs

This work contributed to the MOCSID dataset, an Asilomar 2025 publication on channel charting in the presence of RIS, collaborative project deliverables, and an ongoing first-author journal manuscript.
