## Objective

Grey's Project is a medical-image recognition application designed to distinguish healthy and pathological cases and, when possible, predict a known pathology class.

## Supported imaging modalities

- Optical coherence tomography (OCT).
- Brain magnetic-resonance imaging (MRI).
- Dermoscopic photographs.
- Blood-cell microscopy images.

## Contribution

- Developed a supervised deep-learning workflow for heterogeneous medical-image datasets.
- Contributed to preprocessing, model training, inference and application integration.
- Implemented a usable interface for loading images and displaying predictions.
- Worked collaboratively on code organisation, experimentation and documentation.
