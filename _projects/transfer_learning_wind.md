## Context

Wind farms differ in geography, weather patterns, installed equipment and available historical data. A model trained on one farm therefore does not necessarily transfer effectively to another.

## Work performed

- Reviewed transfer-learning methods for heterogeneous time-series domains.
- Prepared and analysed multi-site wind-power data.
- Designed comparative experiments for multi-horizon prediction.
- Assessed when transferred knowledge improved performance and when domain differences limited it.
- Presented the results to TotalEnergies R&D teams during Mathias Days.

The source code and operational data remain private because this work was completed in an industrial R&D context.
