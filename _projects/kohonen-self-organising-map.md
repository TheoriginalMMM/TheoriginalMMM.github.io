## Objective

Study the theoretical foundations of Kohonen self-organising maps and implement the algorithm to learn topology-preserving representations without labels.

## Work performed

- Implemented the competitive-learning and neighbourhood-update mechanisms.
- Analysed the influence of map structure and training parameters.
- Visualised the evolution of neuron weights for several spatial data distributions.
- Completed the theoretical study, implementation and evaluation independently.
